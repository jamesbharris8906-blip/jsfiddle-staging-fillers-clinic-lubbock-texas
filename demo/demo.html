Botox

Botox and fillers are often mixed up, but they do different jobs. Botox relaxes muscles, so it is best for lines that move, like forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet. Fillers add volume, so they are best for lines that stay put and for spots that have lost fullness. Many people use both, in small amounts, for a balanced result. A visit for Botox takes about fifteen minutes, and most people go straight back to work or class. The change shows up over a few days, and you still look like you. You just look less tired, and your face still moves the way it should.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do cheek and lip fillers last?

Most fillers last six to twelve months, and some last longer in areas that move less. Lips tend to fade faster than cheeks. Your provider will tell you when a touch-up makes sense for you.

Will fillers make my face look puffy?

Not when they are placed well. Small amounts in the right spots lift and support. Overfilling is what looks puffy, and a careful provider will use a light hand and check your face as they go.

What is the downtime after fillers?

Most people go back to work the same day. You may have swelling for a day or two and a small bruise where the needle went in. Ice, gentle care, and no heavy workouts for a day help a lot.

Can fillers be removed if I do not like them?

Many hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with a special enzyme if needed. That is one more reason to pick a trained medical provider who knows how to handle it safely.

Are fillers safe for first-timers?

Fillers are widely used and have a strong safety record when a trained medical provider does them in a clean clinic. Tell your provider about your health, your medicines, and any allergies first.

How much do fillers cost in Lubbock?

Cost depends on the product, the area, and how many syringes you need. A consult gives you a clear price before you commit, so there are no surprises.
